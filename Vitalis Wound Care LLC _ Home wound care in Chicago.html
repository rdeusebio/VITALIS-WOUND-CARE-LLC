What we do

Every plan is built around the patient, from the first evaluation through healing and prevention.

Chronic wound management

Comprehensive, ongoing care for wounds that need long-term attention and monitoring.

  • Comprehensive wound evaluation and treatment initiation
  • Sharp debridement
  • Collagen, cellular and tissue-based products (skin substitutes)
  • Negative pressure wound therapy (Wound VAC)
  • Ultrasonic misting
  • Offloading for diabetic foot ulcers

Pain management

Patient-centered strategies to reduce discomfort and support a more comfortable recovery.

  • Effective pain control strategies tailored to patient needs

Assessment and documentation

  • Infection management

  • Vascular screening
  • Customized dressing selection
  • Wound supply procurement
  • Advanced dressings

Our Clinical Approach

A structured, proven 3-phase methodology tailored to your unique recovery.

Step 01

Evaluate

A full wound evaluation at your home, including exact photographic measurements, depth tracking, infection markers, and vascular screenings.

Step 02

Plan

A personalized treatment plan integrating modern wound supplies, negative pressure, or bioengineered tissue, explained clearly to patient and family.

Step 03

Heal & Prevent

Regular in-home follow-ups, continuous pain monitoring, and lifestyle guidance to accelerate tissue closure and ensure long-term wound prevention.
